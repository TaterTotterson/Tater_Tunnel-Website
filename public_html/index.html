A few good questions

Curious potatoes
welcome.

Do I need my own VPS?

Yes. Tater Tunnel uses a VPS you choose and manage, plus a Home Agent on your home computer. The setup guide covers a fresh Debian or Ubuntu VPS and an existing server.

Do I need to open ports at home?

The Home Agent makes an outbound connection to your VPS, so you do not need inbound port forwarding on your home router. The VPS still needs the ports described in the setup guide.

Does this connect my entire home network?

The default path is for the app routes you choose. It does not give a device unrestricted access to every machine on your network, and it is not a full-internet VPN service.

What can run the Home Agent?

There is a macOS menu bar app, plus Python and Docker options. Remote phones and laptops connect with a WireGuard profile generated by the Home Agent.

What if I lose my phone?

Revoke that device in your Home Agent. This removes its device record and the corresponding WireGuard peer on your VPS. Read about access and security.
